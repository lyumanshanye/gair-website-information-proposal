Research agenda

Study the whole path from data to agency.

Our work asks not only whether a model performs, but why it learns, how it scales and what evidence makes the result reproducible.

Explore the research map →
01

Data & pretraining

Traceable corpora, data refinement and controlled training studies for foundation models.

02

Reasoning & cognition

Methods that turn models from knowledge retrieval systems into thought-construction engines.

03

Agents & environments

Long-horizon interaction, tool use and environment-native learning for dependable agency.

04

Multimodal generation

Unified architectures for generating and understanding text, image, video and audio.

05

Alignment & evaluation

Transparent measures of factuality, safety, preference and real-world capability.

06

AI for research

Systems that discover architectures, datasets and new research directions with people.
