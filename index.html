Research agenda

One lab, six connected areas of intelligence.

Our work spans the full lifecycle of generative systems: what they learn from, how they reason, how we align and evaluate them, and how they act in the world.

Full research archive →
01

Data & pre-training

Scalable methods for curating, refining and understanding the data that shapes foundation models.

02

Reasoning & cognition

Training and inference methods that elicit stronger mathematical, scientific and general reasoning.

03

Alignment & evaluation

Transparent ways to measure factuality, safety, instruction following and human preference.

04

Generative AI for math

Models, datasets and evaluations for mathematical learning, problem solving and discovery.

05

Agents & tools

Models that learn through interaction and turn research capabilities into dependable workflows.

06

Research infrastructure

Open platforms and reproducible tooling that help researchers inspect, compare and improve systems.
