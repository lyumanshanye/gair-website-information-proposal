Research at GAIR

Make intelligence understandable—and buildable.

We treat generative AI as an experimental science. Our work connects data, training, cognition, agency and multimodal generation through hypotheses that can be tested and artifacts that can be reused.

Research map

Six questions, one connected agenda.

The areas below are not organizational silos. Data shapes models; models learn to reason and act; evaluation closes the loop.

01

What should models learn from?

Data & pretraining

Curation, refinement, mixture design, scaling laws and fully traceable pretraining.

02

How does reasoning emerge?

Reasoning & cognition

Data-efficient elicitation, reinforcement learning and test-time cognition engineering.

03

How can models act over long horizons?

Agents & environments

Tool use, authentic interaction trajectories, environment synthesis and software engineering.

04

Can one model perceive and generate many media?

Multimodal generation

Unified modeling of language, vision, video and audio with efficient architectures.

05

How do we know a system is dependable?

Alignment & evaluation

Factuality, safety, preference, benchmark integrity and real-world capability evaluation.

06

Can AI improve the research process itself?

AI for research

Architecture discovery, dataset discovery and agents for innovative research.
