Who we are looking for

We are committed to long-horizon, high-impact research and recruit graduate students who are deeply enthusiastic about AGI. We expect you to be an active thinker, proactive, passionate about research or development, hardworking, unafraid of failure, and genuinely determined to pursue scientific research and real-world applications in large language models. We also continuously recruit students with solid foundations who are still in the early years of undergraduate study, and welcome you to join the group early to explore research.

Important notes

  1. We do not encourage research undertaken merely to publish papers. If your only goal is to obtain a degree, improve job prospects or maximize paper count, please do not choose our group.
  2. Graduate students in the group will be assigned engineering work as needed. If you are unwilling to participate in engineering projects, please do not choose our group.
  3. If you aspire to produce high-quality, high-impact work in frontier areas such as large language models, AI systems, agents, multimodality, AI for science and embodied intelligence, welcome to join us!

Admissions details

(1) Master’s positions

Subject to research funding, during the past three years the group has admitted two academic master’s students, two to three recommended-admission professional master’s students, and approximately ten entrance-exam professional master’s students each year. In daily research we do not distinguish between academic and professional master’s students, and both are eligible to transfer into a doctoral program.

(2) Doctoral positions

Subject to research funding, during the past three years there have been two to three doctoral positions each year.

I recruit direct-entry doctoral students at Shanghai Innovation Institute in joint training with Fudan University, with no quota limit. In addition to passing the Fudan interview, candidates must pass the Shanghai Innovation Institute admission examination. We recommend receiving an offer from Shanghai Innovation Institute before applying to the Fudan summer camp.

(3) Admission routes

  • Summer camp: You must first pass the school’s initial screening. Please contact me after receiving an admission notice for the camp (you may contact me directly for other enquiries). We will arrange a short group interview. Historically, the excellence rate for academic master’s candidates in the summer camp has been around 10%, and the group has two to three academic plus professional master’s positions.
  • Autumn recommended admission: You must first pass the school’s initial screening. Please contact me after receiving an interview notice. We will arrange a group interview. We usually fill all positions during the summer camp and therefore do not recruit in this round.
  • Graduate entrance examination: Please contact me after receiving the second-round examination notice. We will arrange a group interview.

(4) Group interview

We usually arrange a group interview before the school interview. Unlike the school interview, our interview primarily considers research potential, engineering ability, character and teamwork. Students who complete llm-beginner to a high standard are given priority. We especially welcome students who are interested in applying AI algorithms, implementing systems and pursuing problems in depth.

(5) Joining the group early

To help students find more suitable work, the group supports research-related internships at an appropriate stage (usually one to two years) while maintaining research continuity and training quality. The group has ample accelerator resources and offers opportunities to participate in real projects and strengthen engineering skills. Because the first graduate year includes many courses and limited time, students who decide to join are encouraged to begin learning with the group early; recommended-admission students from other universities are encouraged to complete their graduation project at Fudan University (as most previous students have done).

(6) Graduate student support

To create a strong learning and research environment, the group provides competitive graduate stipends in line with the average at comparable research institutions, in addition to university support. Professional master’s students receive some housing support. Students who participate in industry collaboration projects receive additional research support comparable to internships at major technology companies.

(7) Internships

We only encourage internships that are aligned with the research direction and long-term capability development:

  • The group maintains long-term collaborations with leading foundation-model teams. Based on each student’s research performance and development direction, we recommend outstanding students for internships with partner teams. The work should align with the team’s research direction and help students improve their research and engineering capabilities. We usually recommend internships of one to two years, with details decided case by case.
  • Companies founded and led by laboratory alumni also offer internships, and outstanding performers may have opportunities for equity incentives.
  • For research projects where the group cannot temporarily guarantee sufficient compute, we will proactively recommend students to external partner teams for internships.
  • Except during the final year, we do not encourage internships unrelated to the research direction or undertaken only for short-term job seeking.

Before you apply

Make your enquiry easy to evaluate.

A short, specific application is more useful than a generic introduction. The linked role brief remains the authoritative source for requirements.

  • Name the position or collaboration route.
  • Describe the research questions that genuinely interest you.
  • Link to representative papers, code, projects or other evidence of your work.
  • State your institution, current stage and availability.
  • Explain why the fit is specific to GAIR's current work.
