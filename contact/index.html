Before you apply

Make your enquiry easy to evaluate.

A short, specific application is more useful than a generic introduction. The linked role brief remains the authoritative source for requirements.

  • Name the position or collaboration route.
  • Describe the research questions that genuinely interest you.
  • Link to representative papers, code, projects or other evidence of your work.
  • State your institution, current stage and availability.
  • Explain why the fit is specific to GAIR’s current work.
