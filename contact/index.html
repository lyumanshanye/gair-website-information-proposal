Who we are looking for

Do long-horizon work that matters.

We are committed to long-horizon, high-impact research and recruit graduate students who are deeply enthusiastic about AGI. We value active thinkers who take initiative, enjoy research or engineering, work hard, are unafraid of failure, and genuinely aspire to pursue both scientific research and real-world applications in large language models.

We also welcome students with strong foundations who are still in the early years of undergraduate study, and encourage them to begin exploring research with the group as early as possible.

Important notes
  1. 01

    We do not encourage research undertaken merely to publish papers. If your only goal is to obtain a degree, improve job prospects or maximize paper count, please do not choose our group.

  2. 02

    Graduate students in the group will be assigned engineering work as needed. If you are unwilling to participate in engineering projects, please do not choose our group.

Before you apply

Make your enquiry easy to evaluate.

A short, specific application is more useful than a generic introduction. The linked role brief remains the authoritative source for requirements.

  • Name the position or collaboration route.
  • Describe the research questions that genuinely interest you.
  • Link to representative papers, code, projects or other evidence of your work.
  • State your institution, current stage and availability.
  • Explain why the fit is specific to GAIR's current work.
